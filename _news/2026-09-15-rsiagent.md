We released [RSIAgent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15364), a framework for recursive self-improvement in new environments. Curriculum, actor, and verifier agents autonomously explore, verify outcomes, and consolidate experience into memory that guides subsequent rounds. Built on open-source GLM-5.3 and Kimi-K3, it outperforms GPT-6 Astra in partial-credit scores on OSWorld 2.0 and Agents' Last Exam. **[Hugging Face Daily Papers #6](https://huggingface.co/papers/date/2026-09-15)** (Sep 15, 2026); **[Headline feature in Synced (机器之心), an AI news outlet](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/bg0tkvHsKTZ88uBNKUdVTQ)**. See the [code](https://github.com/AetherLabsAI/RSIAgent) and [project page](https://aetherlabsai.github.io/RSIAgent/).
