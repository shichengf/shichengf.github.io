**First author papers: [ICML 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.21135); [NeurIPS 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05524); [COLM 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01687)**

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the [University of Illinois Chicago](https://www.uic.edu/), advised by Prof. [Philip S. Yu](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=D0lL1r0AAAAJ&hl=en). I work closely with Prof. [Kun Zhang](https://www.cmu.edu/dietrich/philosophy/people/faculty/zhang.html) at Carnegie Mellon University and have been a part-time Research Scientist Intern at [**Aether AI**](https://aetherlabs.ai/) since August 2026.

I received my B.Eng. in Automation (Control & Robotics) from [Zhejiang University](https://www.zju.edu.cn/english/)'s [Chu Kochen Honors College](http://ckc.zju.edu.cn/ckcen/), where I worked with Prof. [Qi Ye](https://person.zju.edu.cn/en/yeqi) on dexterous robotic manipulation.

My research explores how **AI agents can recursively improve their own memory, harnesses, and self-evolution mechanisms**. I study how **autonomous exploration and verifiable feedback** help agents accumulate reusable knowledge and skills that guide subsequent exploration and task solving. Recent work includes [**RSIAgent**](https://aetherlabsai.github.io/RSIAgent/) for recursive exploration, [**CoEvoSkills**](https://zhang-henry.github.io/CoEvoSkills/) for skill evolution, and [**Agentic Commerce World**](https://shichengf.github.io/ACWorld/) for evaluating agents in auditable environments.

I welcome research discussions and collaborations. Please feel free to reach out by email.
