First author papers: ICML 2026; NeurIPS 2026; COLM 2026

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Illinois Chicago, advised by Prof. Philip S. Yu. I work closely with Prof. Kun Zhang at Carnegie Mellon University and have been a part-time Research Scientist Intern at Aether AI since August 2026.

I received my B.Eng. in Automation (Control & Robotics) from Zhejiang University’s Chu Kochen Honors College, where I worked with Prof. Qi Ye on dexterous robotic manipulation.

My research explores how AI agents can recursively improve their own memory, harnesses, and self-evolution mechanisms. I study how autonomous exploration and verifiable feedback help agents accumulate reusable knowledge and skills that guide subsequent exploration and task solving. Recent work includes RSIAgent for recursive exploration, CoEvoSkills for skill evolution, and Agentic Commerce World for evaluating agents in auditable environments.

I welcome research discussions and collaborations. Please feel free to reach out by email.
